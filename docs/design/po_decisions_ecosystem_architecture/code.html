PO-09 PENDING PO CONFIRMATION

Incorrect Diagnosis & Contradiction Liability Model

Current Assumption:

If Engineer A quotes a Motherboard replacement (₹4,500) and the customer contests, secondary Engineer B inspects. If Engineer B resolves with an SMPS fuse swap (₹300), Engineer A absorbs a 50% diagnostic reversal penalty deducted from their weekly wallet payout.

Critical PO Question:

Does the platform penalize Engineer A if component misdiagnosis is unintentional vs fraudulent? Who covers Engineer B's travel fee if customer was incorrect?

Impact: High (Payout Engine)
PO-10 PENDING PO CONFIRMATION

Payment Failure & Negative Balance Policy

Current Assumption:

Upon job completion, payment gateway triggers razorpay webhooks. If UPI/Card fails at doorstep, engineer cannot leave until customer pays; after 15 min, job converts to "Debt Outstanding" with engineer receiving base pay credited by platform reserve.

Critical PO Question:

Should the customer account be instantly suspended across phone number and GSTIN if overdue > 48 hours, and does F1 absorb 100% of vendor spare costs upfront?

Impact: Critical (Financial Escrow)
PO-11 PENDING PO CONFIRMATION

Multi-Engineer & AMC Job Assignment & Billing

Current Assumption:

B2B annual maintenance contracts (AMCs) needing multiple workstations serviced concurrently dispatch a "Lead Engineer" who distributes child tickets to 2-3 associate technicians. Lead takes 15% override commission.

Critical PO Question:

How are rating deductions prorated if 1 technician damages hardware while the other 2 complete flawlessly under one single AMC master invoice?

Impact: Medium (B2B Contracts)
PO-13 PENDING PO CONFIRMATION

Customer No-Show Timeout & Travel Visit Charges

Current Assumption:

Engineer arrives, clicks 'Arrived' (geofence verified < 80m). Automated IVR & push ping triggers. If customer is unresponsive after 12 minutes, engineer may mark 'Customer No-Show' with mandatory facade photo.

Critical PO Question:

Is the ₹199 travel convenience fee auto-charged to customer card/wallet, or billed as a block on next booking? What if GPS accuracy is degraded in high-rises?

Impact: High (Field SLA)
PO-04 & PO-05 PENDING PO CONFIRMATION

Engineer Certification Validity & Assessment Rules

Current Assumption:

Apple/Cisco/Microsoft Tier-2 skill badges granted for a flat 365 days. 30 days prior to expiry, app alerts re-certification exam. If failed once, a grace window of 14 days is granted without queue removal.

Critical PO Question:

Should high-risk repairs (e.g. server racks, cleanroom PCB soldering) expire at 6 months instead of 12? Does failed exam immediately degrade tier matching priority?

Impact: Medium (Compliance)
PO-06 & PO-07 PENDING PO CONFIRMATION

Disciplinary Suspension vs Certification Expiry

Current Assumption:

System differentiates `SUSPENDED_DISCIPLINARY` (fraud, code of conduct, rating < 3.8) from `SUSPENDED_ADMIN_EXPIRED` (badge expired). Disciplinary freezes wallet payouts; Admin Expiry permits wallet withdrawal of earned balance.

Critical PO Question:

Can an Operations Supervisor manually override a disciplinary freeze without Head of Operations 2-person signoff?

Impact: High (Legal/Ops)
